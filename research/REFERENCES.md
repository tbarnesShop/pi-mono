# References

## Open-source ATS and adjacent projects

### Forkable or legally reusable as inspiration

- **Reqcore** — https://github.com/reqcore-inc/reqcore
  - License: AGPL-3.0
  - Why it matters: modern ATS architecture, local-first thinking, roadmap includes resume parsing and explainable ranking.

- **YAWIK** — https://github.com/cross-solution/YAWIK
  - License: MIT
  - Why it matters: ATS + job board, older but permissive and directly in the recruiting domain.

- **OpenCATS** — https://github.com/opencats/OpenCATS
  - License: dual licensing notes in repo; OpenCATS code under MPL 2.0, legacy CATS code under modified MPL terms
  - Why it matters: classic ATS domain model and workflows.

- **FreeATS** — https://github.com/freeats/freeats
  - License: MIT
  - Why it matters: modern-ish self-hosted ATS with a simple deployment story.

- **SpotAxis** — https://github.com/Assystant/SpotAxis
  - License: MIT
  - Why it matters: another open ATS reference, but much lighter-weight.

### Strongly copyleft or platform-scale references

- **Ever Gauzy** — https://github.com/ever-co/ever-gauzy
  - License: AGPL-3.0
  - Why it matters: ATS inside a broad business platform; useful for feature breadth and org model.

- **Frappe HRMS** — https://github.com/frappe/hrms
  - License: GPL-3.0
  - Why it matters: recruitment inside a mature HR suite.

### Not safe to copy

- **openats** — https://github.com/lukegalea/openats
  - No visible repo license file; do not copy code without explicit permission.
